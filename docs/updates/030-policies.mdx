## Policies

Nuon now supports **Policies** for component deployments, sandbox runs, and external container images — extending beyond the existing in-cluster Kyverno policies to give you control over your entire deployment pipeline.

### What's New

- **Deploy-time policies**: Validate Terraform plans, Helm charts, and Kubernetes manifests before changes are applied. Block deployments with `deny` rules or log warnings with `warn` rules
- **Sandbox policies**: Enforce standards on sandbox infrastructure provisioning
- **External image policies**: Validate container image metadata during builds — check for signed images, SBOMs, and attestations before they reach your installs
- **Policy reporting**: View and filter all policy evaluations across installs by status, type, and more — useful for compliance auditing and tracking policy adherence over time
