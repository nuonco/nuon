---
title: "Portal"
description: "Customers can self-serve app installs through your portal"
---

First, your customer will select a provided app by clicking "view."

![App catalog](../images/concepts/portal/app-catalog.png)

Once inside the app, in the top right corner, they'll click "install."

![Install button](../images/concepts/portal/install-button.png)

A form will pop-up where your customer can enter any inputs.

![Install form](../images/concepts/portal/install-form.png)

Once filled out, they'll click "create install," and their install will start deploying.

![Provisioning install](../images/concepts/portal/provisioning-install.png)
