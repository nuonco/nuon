An app is your template for creating [installs](/concepts/installs) in a customer's account. You map your existing
application, Nuon makes it deployable into any customer's
environment.

## What you map

- **[Components](/concepts/components)**: the Helm charts, Terraform modules, Kubernetes manifests, and container
  images you already use.
- **[Inputs](/concepts/app-inputs)** and **[secrets](/concepts/app-secrets)**: what changes per customer, such as
  region or an API key.

The app lives as TOML in your own git repo. The [configuration reference](/config-ref) covers every file.

<Tip>
Change your app any time. Push to git, and an [app branch](/concepts/app-branches) rolls the change out to your
installs.
</Tip>
