Package your product once as a Nuon App Template. Nuon uses that template to create installs of your app in your customer's cloud.

Your app template is a set of TOML files, the [app config](/configuration-files), in your own git repo. Most teams
keep a few, one per flavor of BYOC, such as AWS and Azure.

## [Installs](/concepts/installs)

An install is one instance of your App Template in a customer's cloud account.

and your components deploy on top.

## [Updates and Operations](/concepts/updates-and-operations)

Every change to an install runs as a [workflow](/concepts/workflows) with a plan you can review, checked against your
[policies](/concepts/policies).
