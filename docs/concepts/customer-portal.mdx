The Customer Portal is Nuon's hosted web app where your customers install, monitor, and manage their own installs. Each vendor org gets a branded portal at `https://<your-org-name>.customers.nuon.co`.

## Why use the Customer Portal

The portal shifts install management from a vendor-operated workflow to a customer self-service workflow:

- **Shorter time to value.** Customers install in minutes without waiting on the vendor.
- **Scales with your pipeline.** Vendors can serve more customers without linearly growing support headcount.
- **Branded experience.** The portal lives under your subdomain and carries your branding.
- **Customer retains control.** Customers approve provisioning, update inputs, and manage their install on their terms.

## What customers can do

From the portal, customers can:

- Browse the catalog of apps you publish to them
- Install an app into their own cloud account
- Supply any `user_configurable` [inputs](/concepts/app-inputs) during install
- Approve provisioning and subsequent changes
- Watch health checks and install status

Vendors control which apps a customer sees, which inputs they can edit, and which approval style applies — one-click "approve all" versus step-by-step review.

## What the vendor still controls

Installs created through the portal are first-class installs. Vendors see them in the [web app](/concepts/web-app) and can reach them via [CLI](/concepts/cli) and [API](/concepts/api) the same way as vendor-created installs.
