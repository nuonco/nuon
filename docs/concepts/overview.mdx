Package your product once as a Nuon App Template. Nuon uses that template to create installs of your app in your customer's cloud.

## [App Templates](/concepts/apps)

An App Template is your product, described as TOML in your own git repo. You provide two things:

Most teams keep a few templates, one per flavor of BYOC, such as AWS and Azure.

## Nuon Infrastructure

What Nuon runs in each customer's cloud account so your product can run there.

<FlowSteps
  steps={[
    { title: "Stack", detail: "The customer applies it: network, permissions, and a VM for the runner" },
    { title: "Runner", detail: "Starts on that VM and does every deploy, action, and runbook" },
    { title: "Sandbox", detail: "Base compute your components deploy into, such as a Kubernetes cluster" },
  ]}
/>

Read more about the [stack](/concepts/stacks), the [runner](/concepts/runners), and the [sandbox](/concepts/sandboxes).
The stack can create a [new VPC](/concepts/stacks/stand-alone-vpc), or deploy into the
[customer's VPC](/concepts/stacks/customer-vpc) or [cluster](/concepts/stacks/customer-cluster).
