This section covers the core primitives you need to understand to use Nuon.

- **[Apps & Installs](/concepts/apps-and-installs)** - Apps define your product. Installs deploy it into customer cloud accounts.
- **[Stacks](/concepts/stacks)** - The cloud-native template (CloudFormation, Bicep) that bootstraps an install with networking and the Runner.
- **[Runners](/concepts/runners)** - The agent deployed into each install that executes jobs from the control plane.
- **[Sandboxes](/concepts/sandboxes)** - The base infrastructure layer (networking, compute) provisioned in each install.
- **[Components](/concepts/components)** - The individual pieces of your application: Helm charts, Terraform modules, manifests, and container images.
- **[App Configuration](/concepts/app-configuration)** - Inputs, variables, and secrets that parameterize your app across installs.
- **[Workflows](/concepts/workflows)** - The lifecycle steps for deploying, updating, or tearing down an install.
- **[Actions](/concepts/actions)** - Reusable operational tasks like health checks, migrations, and maintenance scripts.
- **[Policies](/concepts/policies)** - OPA and Kyverno rules that enforce compliance and security standards during builds and deploys.
- **[Customer Experience](/concepts/customer-portal)** - How your customers interact with installs, from the hosted portal to your own product integration.
