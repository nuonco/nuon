You define your app once. Each customer runs a copy in their own cloud account.

<PlatformDiagram />

- **[App templates](/concepts/apps)**: your product as TOML in your git repo. It lists your
  [components](/concepts/components), plus the [inputs](/concepts/app-inputs) and [secrets](/concepts/app-secrets)
  that change per customer.
- **[Installs](/concepts/installs)**: one copy of your app template in a customer's cloud account. The customer applies
  the [stack](/concepts/stacks) with their own credentials, and the [runner](/concepts/runners) deploys the
  [sandbox](/concepts/sandboxes) and your components.
- **[Updates and operations](/concepts/updates-and-operations)**: push to git and an
  [app branch](/concepts/app-branches) rolls the change out in order, with a plan you can review first.
  [Actions](/concepts/actions) and [runbooks](/concepts/runbooks) handle migrations and day-2 work.

## How the pieces relate

An app template has app branches. Each app branch splits its installs into install groups that deploy in order.
