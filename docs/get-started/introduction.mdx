## What problems does Nuon solve?

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Deploy into different customer environments">
    Every customer is different. Some want BYOVPC, others need BYOLLM or bring-your-own-bucket. Nuon lets you deploy the same app into diverse customer environments—turning pets into cattle.

    One app. Many environments. Zero forks.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Continuously update every customer">
    Push updates to all customer deployments from a single pipeline. Nuon handles approvals, drift detection, and rollbacks. You ship updates. Your customer approves them.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Give your customer control">
    Your customers see exactly what's deployed in their cloud. They control update timing, approve changes, and maintain full audit trails. Managed by you, controlled by your customer.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
